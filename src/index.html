August 2027 · University of Sheffield · for Dig Deep

Three friends, one mountain, and a lot of reasons to keep walking uphill.

In August 2027, Aaron, Kobie and Lui, three final-year students at the University of Sheffield, leave their dissertations, their warm beds and their excuses at the trailhead of the Machame Route and walk uphill for seven days.

Kilimanjaro is 5,895 metres of scree, ice and thin air. The final push starts at midnight, in the dark, at minus ten, with the summit somewhere above the clouds and nobody guaranteeing we get there.

We're doing it because 703 million people still live without clean water close to home. In Bomet County, Kenya, two in three people go without, and every pound we raise helps Dig Deep change that.

03 — The cause

Water in Bomet County, Kenya.

Dig Deep works alongside communities in Bomet County, Kenya, protecting springs and building the water, toilets and hygiene services families need, together with local businesses and the Kenyan government.

Here, two in three people lack clean water, safe sanitation and good hygiene. Waterborne disease is one of the leading killers of children under five. Long walks for water keep children out of school and parents out of work, and the burden falls hardest on women and girls.
